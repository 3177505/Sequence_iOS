### Short info to print (or handwrite) beside the images

**About the artist (Jon Rafman, b. 1981)**  
Rafman works across video, installation, photography and internet archives, often treating online image-worlds as both documentary material and psychological space.

**Key statement (use as a caption / wall text)**  
From an interview excerpt reproduced in KQED, Rafman describes his concern as:  
“What concerns me is the general sense of entrapment and isolation felt by many as social and political life becomes increasingly abstracted and experience dematerialized.”  
Source: KQED, “9-Eyes is a Journey Around the World, One Strange Snapshot at a Time” (updated Sep 18, 2024): `https://www.kqed.org/arts/13880898/9-eyes-is-a-journey-around-the-world-one-strange-snapshot-at-a-time`

**Exhibition statement excerpt (ties directly to Image 2)**  
Bernheim’s exhibition overview describes *Egregore* (2021) as animated found images that “merge and transform,” and says the work “excavat[es] the collective unconscious of the web” and a “desire to search for meaning.”  
Source: Bernheim gallery exhibition text: `https://www.bernheimgallery.com/exhibitions/36-and-grimoires-jon-rafman/`
